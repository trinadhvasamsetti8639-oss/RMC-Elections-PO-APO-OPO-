Assign Reserve Staff to Polling Station

Only reserve staff who have attended can be assigned. Others are listed as unavailable – not assigned due to not present.

Geofencing

Add a Google Maps key in config.js to pick the point on a map. Typing latitude/longitude works without it.

Database & reports

Save the employee list once (and after any change). Live reports with names are in Supabase > Table Editor > Views: report_team, report_summary, report_reserve (export to CSV there). The audit_log table keeps every action. Clearing the entire database is disabled for safety.

Employee database (Update / Merge only)

Use the same sheet format: polling station sheet (GROUP_NO, PO_ELECT_IDNO…) and/or reserve sheet (EID, POST_dESG…). Existing attendance records are always preserved.
